The AIR group conducts cutting-edge research in artificial intelligence, robotics, material intelligence, and signal processing. The missions of the team are:

    •    to carry out original research on artificial intelligence and signal processing and their applications;

    •    to act as a strong research base to foster international collaborations;

    •    to transfer academic research results into practical uses to benefit the industrial, commercial, and service sectors.

The group applies AI, robotics, and signal processing to

    •    Computer vision, 3D scene analysis, and 3D object recognition (Prof. Kenneth Lam, Prof. L.P. Chau, Prof. Luca Rossi, and Dr. Yi Wang)

    •    Spoken language processing, voice biometrics, voice privacy, and speech for healthcare (Prof. K.A. Lee and Dr. W.W. Lin)

    •    Bioinformatics and biomedical engineering (Prof. Bonnie Law)

    •    Image and video processing (Prof. Y.L. Chan)

    •    Material intelligence (Prof. H.T. Zhao and Dr. Z.D. He)
